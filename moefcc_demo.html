1

Download PDFs

Fetches all 40 annual reports from moef.gov.in using urllib with a polite crawl delay. Saves to reports_pdf/.

2

Extract Text

Primary: PyMuPDF (fitz) for fast, accurate PDF parsing. Fallback: pdfminer.six for complex/scanned PDFs. Handles multi-column layouts from older reports.

3

Clean & Segment

De-hyphenation, whitespace normalisation, non-ASCII removal. Sentence segmentation via spaCy en_core_web_sm with regex fallback. Filters sentences shorter than 40 chars.

4

Seed Labelling (Keyword Rules)

Each sentence scored against 5 keyword dictionaries (25 keywords per theme). Best-matching theme wins. Confidence = hits / 5, capped at 1.0. Unlabelled sentences filtered out.

5

Save Dataset CSV

Columns: sentence, theme, confidence, report_year, source_file. Expected yield: ~80,000–200,000 labelled rows across 40 reports spanning 1986–2026.

6

TF-IDF + LogReg Baseline

Trigram TF-IDF (50K features, sublinear TF) + Multinomial LogReg. 80/20 train-test split stratified by theme. Typical baseline F1: 0.82–0.88 on seed-labelled data.

7

Transformer Fine-Tuning (Production)

distilbert-base-uncased fine-tuned for 5 epochs on GPU with early stopping. Expected F1: 0.91–0.94. Swap for ai4bharat/indic-bert for Hindi-language reports.

Theme Coverage Strategy
Seed labels are generated via keyword matching, which gives ~70–80% precision. Manual review of a random 500-sentence sample is recommended before fine-tuning. For best results, augment with sentences from IPCC reports, CBD documents, and UNFCCC NDCs to improve coverage of climate_adaptation and eco_friendly_practices.

Inference example

from moefcc_nlp_pipeline import SustainabilityClassifier

clf = SustainabilityClassifier("baseline_model.pkl")
results = clf.classify([
    "The National Wetlands Conservation Programme aims to protect and manage wetland ecosystems.",
    "India submitted its Nationally Determined Contribution to reduce emissions intensity.",
    "Soil organic carbon levels in degraded lands were restored through afforestation.",
])
for r in results:
    print(r["theme"], r["confidence"], r["sentence"][:60])
